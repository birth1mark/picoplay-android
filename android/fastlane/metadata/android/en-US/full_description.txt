PicoPlay is an open-source, high-performance fantasy console player engineered for Android smartphones, tablets, handhelds, and TV.

Play your own 128x128 8-bit fantasy console cartridges (.p8 and .p8.png) with native 60 FPS performance, low-latency audio, and responsive on-screen controls.

KEY FEATURES:
• Ergonomic Pocket Touch Gamepad: Directional virtual joystick with cardinal axis gating and tactile circular action buttons.
• External Gamepad Support: Full auto-detection for Bluetooth and USB gamepads (PlayStation, Xbox, 8BitDo, and retro handhelds). On-screen controls automatically hide upon controller input.
• Storage Access Framework: Easily import your own cartridges (.p8 and .p8.png) from your device storage, SD Card, or cloud drive.
• 3 Display Modes: Switch seamlessly between MAX (edge-to-edge), FIT (framed handheld bezel), and 1:1 (pixel-perfect integer scaling).
• Zero Telemetry: Completely private with zero data collection, no background tracking, and no ads.
