Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PicoPlay Android.

What is PicoPlay Android?
PicoPlay Android is an open-source, native C++ fantasy console player and pocket arcade frontend for Android devices. It runs 128×128 8-bit PICO-8 cartridges at 60 FPS with hardware-accelerated SDL2 graphics, low-latency OpenSL ES audio, touch controls, and physical gamepad support.
How do I load my own custom cartridges (.p8 or .p8.png)?
Tap the [+CART] button at the top header of the screen at any time. This invokes the native Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) file picker, allowing you to load any .p8 text cart or .p8.png graphic cartridge from your Downloads folder, SD card, or cloud storage.
Does PicoPlay support Bluetooth and USB gamepads?
Yes! PicoPlay includes automatic controller detection for PlayStation DualShock 4/DualSense, Xbox Wireless, 8BitDo controllers, and dedicated retro handhelds (such as Retroid Pocket, AYN Odin, and Anbernic). The on-screen touch controls automatically hide when a physical controller button is pressed.
How do mouse-driven cartridges (like Slipways) work?
PicoPlay features real-time touch-to-mouse translation. When playing mouse-compatible games like Slipways or Dungeonmans, simply touch and drag directly inside the gameplay screen to control the in-game cursor and click planets or menus with zero extra configuration.
Is PicoPlay ready for Android 16 and 16KB memory pages?
Yes. PicoPlay is compiled with modern Android NDK toolchains and conforms to 16KB ELF alignment and Target SDK 36 (Android 16), ensuring compatibility with next-generation Android devices.

Official Privacy Policy

Application: com.birth1mark.picoplay • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

PicoPlay does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal user data, device identifiers, precise or approximate location, analytics, or crash telemetry. The application operates completely offline without user accounts, logins, or tracking.

2. Local File Handling

Cartridge files loaded through the native Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) remain strictly on your local device within the application's isolated private internal storage. No user files ever leave your device.

3. Permissions

• android.permission.VIBRATE: Exclusively used for tactile haptic feedback when pressing on-screen gamepad buttons.
• The Google Play Store edition operates 100% offline, requests zero network permissions, and connects to no external servers.

4. Zero Advertisements & No Third-Party SDKs

PicoPlay contains zero advertisements, zero advertising IDs, zero analytics SDKs, and zero in-app purchases. It is fully compliant with COPPA, GDPR, and Google Play Families policies.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries, visit the official open-source repository at github.com/birth1mark/picoplay-android.
